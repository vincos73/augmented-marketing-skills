# Marketing Foundations — Relaybird

Do not present it as payroll, scheduling, compliance, or employee monitoring. If chain size or use situation is unclear, ask before asserting fit. `[C; S1]`
- Introduce Relaybird Launch only as a current implementation service when the buyer lacks rollout capacity. `[C; S1]`

## 2. Messaging, Claims & Evidence Rules
