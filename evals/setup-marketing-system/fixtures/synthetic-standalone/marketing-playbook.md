# Relaybird marketing playbook — synthetic

## Stable audience and situation

Lead with Relaybird Core for operations directors at retail chains with 20–150 stores when they need to roll out new procedures, open several locations, or correct inconsistent execution. Relaybird Launch may be introduced when the buyer lacks rollout capacity. Do not market Relaybird as a payroll, scheduling, compliance, or employee-monitoring product.

## Stable messages

- Make procedure rollout visible without removing store-level accountability.
- Help regional teams see who has acknowledged a procedure and where follow-up is needed.
- Keep the language practical; do not promise transformation without operational detail.

## Writing standard

Prefer concrete verbs and short sentences. Explain what changes in the manager's work. Avoid hype, fear about employee performance, and generic claims that AI “runs the business.”

## Open issue

The playbook does not define whether named competitor comparisons are allowed.
