# Relaybird claims and proof register — synthetic

## Approved product facts

- Relaybird Core publishes procedures and records acknowledgements. Product documentation version 4.2. Public use allowed.
- Relaybird Launch is a current fixed-scope onboarding service. Service brief dated 2026-07-15. Public use allowed.

## Outcome study

A three-customer pilot measured median time from assigning an onboarding module to recorded completion. The median improvement was 18% over eight weeks. The sample covered 31 stores and did not measure employee productivity, retention, compliance, or financial return.

The Marketing Director allows the 18% result only when the sample, metric, and pilot scope are stated. Customer names cannot be disclosed.

## Unsupported website number

The 35% figure came from the best-performing store in the pilot. It is not approved as a general or representative claim and must be removed from new materials.
