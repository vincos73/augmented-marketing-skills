# Relaybird quality and approval policy — synthetic

## Minimum quality checks

- Confirm that every offer is current in the business identity.
- Verify every numerical, comparative, or customer outcome claim against the claims register.
- Use descriptive alt text for informative images and captions for prerecorded marketing video.
- Use the official product names and avoid surveillance framing.

## Authority

- Agents may analyze sources, recommend approaches, and create internal drafts.
- The Marketing Director approves public marketing content.
- Customer Success and the customer must approve named logos, testimonials, or customer-identifying stories.
- Legal review is required for compliance claims, named competitor comparisons, and new contract language.
- Approval of copy does not authorize publication, email sending, account changes, media activation, or spend.

The owner for approving an unnamed comparative claim is not defined.
