# Marketing Foundations overlay — Fieldnote

Only differences from Copperline Group Marketing Foundations appear here.

## Messaging, Claims & Evidence Rules

- “Plastic-free retail packaging” is approved only for the current EU range. Do not shorten it to “plastic-free” or extend it to the product. `[C]`
- “Zero-waste,” “fully sustainable,” and equivalent absolute claims are prohibited. `[C]`

## Channel & Format Roles

- Fieldnote Instagram supports product-in-use stories, work rituals, and gifting inspiration. It is not a customer-support channel. `[C]`

## Editorial, Visual & Quality Standards

- Use warm, specific language and show notebooks in real work settings. Avoid corporate procurement language and unsupported environmental symbolism. `[C]`

## Controls, Authority & Approvals

- Fieldnote Brand Lead approves public brand content. Sustainability Lead also reviews environmental claims. Publication requires separate authorization. `[C]`
