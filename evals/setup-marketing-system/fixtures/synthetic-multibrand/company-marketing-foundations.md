# Marketing Foundations — Copperline Group

## Shared rules

- Apply product, audience, and availability facts only after reading the relevant child identity. `[C]`
- Use environmental claims only when tied to a documented attribute or scoped lifecycle result. Absolute sustainability claims are prohibited. `[C]`
- Corporate LinkedIn supports company news and employer communication; it is not the default product-marketing channel for every child brand. `[C]`
- Every public output requires the relevant Brand Lead's content approval. Environmental claims additionally require Sustainability Lead review. `[C]`
- Content approval does not authorize publication, paid distribution, account changes, or spend. `[C]`

## Quality

- Use the child brand's approved language and visual references.
- Verify product availability, material claim scope, image rights, and accessibility before review.

## Review triggers

- Review affected rules when brand relationships, materials, markets, evidence, or approval roles change.
