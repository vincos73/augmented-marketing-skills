# Fieldnote marketing guide — synthetic source

Instagram should show the notebook in daily design work and gifting moments. The tone is warm and observant, never corporate or procurement-led.

An old brainstorm used the headline “the zero-waste gift for creative teams.” This line was rejected after material review because the notebook contains metal and synthetic adhesive. The approved environmental language is limited to “plastic-free retail packaging” for the current EU range.

The Fieldnote Brand Lead reviews brand content. The Sustainability Lead reviews environmental claims. Neither approval by itself authorizes publication.
